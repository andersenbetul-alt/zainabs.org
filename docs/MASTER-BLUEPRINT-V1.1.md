# ZAINAB.ORG — Master Blueprint V1.1
24 November 2026 — Concept, not operational approval.
## 1 Brand
ZAINAB, Purple Hope, dedicated to Zeynep Öner. Vision: nobody affected by cancer should stand alone. Mission: accessible knowledge, support navigation and solidarity. Values: dignity, hope, care, trust, equality, inclusion. NO: Håp. Omsorg. Fellesskap. TR: Umut. Şefkat. Dayanışma. EN: Hope. Care. Together.
## 2 Design system
Nordic Humanitarian Design; Swiss Grid 12/8/4 columns, 8px baseline, 1280px maximum, 24px gutter, spacing 8/16/24/32/48/64/96px. Interactive grid overlay and alignment verification required. Playfair Display and Manrope. Preserve approved logo geometry; approval and source asset must be verified.
Colors: #452568 primary, #7851A9 secondary, #AA8BC8 lavender, #E2D6EF surface, #F5F0FA mist, #FCFAFD white, #C6A16E gold, #28212D text, #2D1946 footer.
## 3 Art direction
Hope (garden conversation), Care (support conversation), Family (home), Everyday (practical help), Kids (creative activities), Knowledge (research), Life (nature), Remembrance (lavender garden), Community (volunteers), Work (working life). Licensed or original illustrations, responsive WebP/AVIF, accurate alt text; never imply illustrative people are actual patients.
## 4 Planned service ecosystem
Care: practical everyday assistance; Family: caregiver resources; Kids & Youth: age-appropriate resources; Support: navigation; Rights: public services and benefits; Life: life during and after cancer; Work: accommodation and return to work; Knowledge: verified information; Community: moderated solidarity; Academy: education; Volunteer: screening and training; Global: multilingual resources; Donate: conditional on legal and financial approval; Memorial: authorized remembrance content. All proposed, not verified active services.

Primary navigation NO/TR/EN: Hjem/Home/Ana sayfa; Om kreft/About Cancer/Kanser hakkında; Finn støtte/Find Support/Destek bulun; For pårørende/For Families/Hasta yakınları; Våre tilbud/Our Services/Hizmetlerimiz; Kunnskap/Knowledge/Bilgi merkezi; Fellesskap/Community/Dayanışma; Engasjer deg/Get Involved/Katılım; Om ZAINAB/About ZAINAB/ZAINAB hakkında; Kontakt/Contact/İletişim.
Routes: /, /om-kreft, /krefttyper, /symptomer-og-diagnose, /behandling, /pasientstotte, /parorende, /barn-og-unge, /hverdagsstotte, /rettigheter, /okonomisk-stotte, /arbeid-og-helse, /livet-etter-kreft, /finn-hjelp, /fellesskap, /kurs, /arrangementer, /forskning, /aktuelt, /historier, /bli-frivillig, /stott-oss, /samarbeid, /om-oss, /zeynep-oner, /apenhet, /kontakt, /personvern, /tilgjengelighet, /informasjonskapsler, /faq, /sok, /min-side, /nettbutikk. Last two and donation gated.
## 6 Homepage
Utility bar, logo, navigation, language and search; hero Ingen skal stå alene; six needs cards; verified support directory; service categories; family and youth; knowledge and rights; work; articles; community; remembrance; engagement; footer. No invented contact details or credentials.
## 7 Subpages
Patient: diagnosis, treatment resources, support, rights. Family: caregivers, children, care, daily life. Youth: age-appropriate content for children, teens, parents. Memorial: authorized story, values, purpose. Shared navigation and accessible layouts.
## 8 Service blueprint
Stages: need, discovery, information, referral, feedback. Frontstage: web, search, guide, directory, feedback. Backstage: research, UX, review, source verification, quality. Systems: CMS, search, content, directory, privacy-friendly analytics. No health data collection in MVP.
## 9 Audiences
Patients at diagnosis, treatment and survivorship; family, youth, workers, volunteers, supporters and professionals. Minimize steps to useful resources.
## 10 Platform
P0 public site, search, knowledge, directory, languages, CMS. P1 admin, stories, newsletter, events. P2 source-grounded AI, moderated community, volunteers, donations. P3 shop, accounts, mobile app. AI never diagnoses or prescribes. Future modules require legal, safety and operational review.
## 11 Engineering
Inspect existing repository; preserve history and approved assets. Modular assets/brand, images, css, js, pages, locales, content, docs, tests. Semantic HTML, responsive layout, one token source, keyboard navigation, SEO and QA. Work on design/purple-hope; production approval separate.
## 12 Content
NO/TR/EN editorial consistency; sources, dates, review ownership. No fabricated stories. SEO: canonical, hreflang, sitemap, robots, semantic headings, structured data only for verified facts, performance.
## 13 Governance
GDPR and data minimization; NIST/NSM risk-based controls; HTTPS, MFA, RBAC, logging and backups where applicable. WCAG 2.2 AA target, not certification. Safeguarding and moderation required before community launch. No donations or payments until authorization. No medical credentials, statistics or partnerships without verification.
## 14 Team capabilities
Strategy, ResearchOps, service design, brand, UX/UI, engineering, content, medical review, security/privacy, accessibility, analytics, QA, community and partnerships. Proposed responsibilities, not staffed roles.
## 15 Roadmap
Discover → Define → Design → Build → Validate → Launch → Measure → Scale. V1 public site and resources; V1.2 languages; V1.3 CMS/search; V2 gated AI/community/volunteers/events; later approved donations/shop and portal/app.
## 16 Development instruction
Implement original independent identity, preserve approved assets, apply tokens and grids, build responsive accessible pages, verify source links and images, document privacy/security and tests, deliver PR for review. Do not deploy without explicit approval.
## 17 Acceptance checklist
Brand/logo, tokens, homepage and subpages, images/licenses, translations, content review, navigation/search/CMS, responsive and browser tests, keyboard/contrast, grid verification, broken links, performance, privacy/security, verified service status, change log and release approval.
## 18 Manifesto
Kanser bir insanın bütün kimliği değildir. Her insanın bir hikâyesi, ailesi, hayalleri ve yaşamı vardır. ZAINAB umudun, bilginin, şefkatin ve dayanışmanın buluştuğu bir yer olmayı amaçlar. Hope. Care. Together.
